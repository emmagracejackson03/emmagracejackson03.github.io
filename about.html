About Me

Revit Certified • Team Collaborator • IIDA Member

My Journey

Hi! I'm Emma Jackson, a Revit-certified Interior Design student completing my bachelor's degree at Weber State University. Born and raised in Utah, I’ve always been drawn to the way spaces shape how we live. I still remember my dad catching me rearranging my bedroom in the middle of the night—because I wanted to make it more functional. That impulse to optimize and reimagine environments has stayed with me.

With professional experience as a BIM drafter at Spectrum Engineers, I bring both technical expertise and creative vision to every project. Working at the intersection of engineering and interior design, I offer a holistic understanding of how buildings come together—from structural systems to spatial aesthetics. I believe great design starts with knowing how each piece fits, functions, and contributes to the whole.

My Design Philosophy

"Great interior design goes beyond aesthetics - it's about creating spaces that genuinely enhance how people live, work, and feel."
